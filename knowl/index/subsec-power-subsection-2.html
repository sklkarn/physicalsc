The rate of doing work is called power . It is also the rate at which the energy can be transferred. Power tell us that how fast the work has been done. Power can be expressed as
\begin{equation} P =\frac{W}{t}\tag{3.1.6} \end{equation}
\begin{align*} or, P \amp = \frac{Fd}{t} \\ or, P \amp = F\frac{d}{t} \\ \therefore P \amp = F v \end{align*}
Its SI unit is Watt (w=j/s).
