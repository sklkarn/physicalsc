caused by the gravitational pull of the moon and sun on Earth’s oceans. The moon’s gravity pulls water outward, creating a bulge that moves as the moon orbits Earth. The moon has a stronger effect on tides than the sun due to its closer distance. When the sun and moon are aligned, tides are more extreme, higher highs and lower lows, called Spring Tides. When the sun and moon are at right angles, tides are weaker than usual, called Neap Tides. Every 24 hours, there are always two high tides and two low tides.
